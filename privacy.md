# Privacy Policy — Bumble Bee Tuner

_Last updated: April 13, 2026_

## Overview

Bumble Bee Tuner ("the App") is developed by TopShelfTahoe. This privacy policy describes how the App handles your data.

**The short version: we don't collect any data. Your audio never leaves your device.**

## Microphone Usage

The App requires microphone access to detect the pitch of your instrument. Audio from the microphone is:

- Processed entirely on your device in real time
- Used only for pitch detection while the App is active
- Never recorded, stored, or saved to disk
- Never transmitted to any server, third party, or external service

When you close the App or stop the tuner, microphone access ends immediately.

## Data Collection

Bumble Bee Tuner does **not** collect, store, or transmit:

- Personal information
- Audio recordings
- Usage analytics
- Location data
- Device identifiers
- Any other user data

## Third-Party Services

The App does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

## Apple Watch

The Apple Watch companion app performs its own on-device pitch detection using the Watch microphone. The same privacy principles apply: audio is processed locally and never stored or transmitted.

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new "Last updated" date.
