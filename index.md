# BumbleBee Tuner

A precision strobe tuner for iPhone and iPad with a fully standalone Apple
Watch app. Raise your wrist, play a note, feel the buzz.

**[Download on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/bumblebee-tuner/id6805751115)**

Free. No ads, no subscriptions, no accounts, no analytics.

## What it does

- **A real strobe.** The strobe drifts while you're off pitch, yellow when
  flat and orange when sharp, and settles green when you're there.
- **Rock-steady tracking.** A YIN pitch engine with octave-error protection.
  The needle sits still on a held note and snaps when you turn a peg.
- **Standalone Apple Watch tuner.** The Watch listens with its own mic, no
  iPhone needed, and taps your wrist the moment you lock in. Turn the Digital
  Crown to boost sensitivity when the instrument is at arm's length.
- **7 instruments, 17 tunings.** Guitar (Standard, Drop D, Half and Full Step
  Down, Open G, Open D, Open E, Double Drop D, DADGAD), bass (EADG, Drop D,
  5-string BEADG), ukulele, mandolin, violin, cello, banjo. Or free chromatic.
- **The details.** Reference pitch A4 = 432 to 444 Hz, live cents readout with
  a pitch trail, strobe or needle-bar display, haptic confirmation on iPhone
  and Watch.

## Apple Watch tip

To keep the tuner on screen with your wrist down: Settings › Display &
Brightness › Always On › Show Apps, include BumbleBee Tuner, and set
General › Return to Clock to anything other than Always.
